- One H1 per page
- Unique titles and descriptions
- 100% alt coverage

## Findings
| Severity | Finding | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Missing og:image on 18 pages | Page-level openGraph without images overrides the layout image. | Add images to each openGraph. |
| Medium | Long titles | 15 titles over 60 chars; blog titles 81-100. | Shorten. |
| Medium | H1 missing space | Homepage H1 text 'Build AI thatworks in the real world.' | Add a space before <br />. |
| Medium | Weak internal linking | Blog posts 2 inbound links, some service and location pages 1. | Add latest-articles strip, related links, footer links. |
| Low | Long meta descriptions | 7 descriptions over 160 chars. | Trim to 140-155. |
